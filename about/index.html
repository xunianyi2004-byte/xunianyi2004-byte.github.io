ABOUT US

OUR STORY

24 years devoted to better flameless candles.

Ningbo Langsheng Artware Co., Ltd. was established in 2002. Since then, we have focused on the development and manufacture of LED flameless candles for indoor, outdoor, hospitality and seasonal collections.

Design, production, testing, packing and export support come together under one roof—giving customers a direct, responsive and practical manufacturing partner.
